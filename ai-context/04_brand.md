# Vivabox — Brand Model

## Brand Positioning

Vivabox is a premium accessible brand that offers experience gifts.

The brand allows people to offer meaningful and memorable gifts without the stress of choosing a specific activity.

Vivabox transforms the uncertainty of choosing a gift into a simple and reliable solution.

Brand promise:

"El regalo que siempre acierta."

---

# Brand Personality

Vivabox should feel:

Elegant  
Warm  
Modern  
Human  

The brand must never feel:

- corporate
- bureaucratic
- technical
- complicated

Vivabox should feel like a thoughtful and intelligent gift solution.

---

# Emotional Goal

When someone discovers Vivabox, they should think:

"Qué buena idea de regalo."

When someone receives a Vivabox, they should feel:

Sorpresa  
Emoción  
Curiosidad  

The emotional sequence is:

Gift → Surprise → Experience

---

# Target Audience

Vivabox targets people looking for thoughtful gifts.

Typical buyers include:

- urban professionals
- couples
- friends and family members
- companies offering employee gifts

The brand is designed for middle and upper-middle class consumers in Colombia.

---

# Brand Voice

Vivabox communication should be:

Clear  
Warm  
Simple  
Positive  

Language should always feel human and natural.

Avoid complex or technical language.

Short sentences are preferred.

---

# Copywriting Principles

Always speak to the buyer.

The buyer is looking for reassurance that the gift will succeed.

Communication should focus on:

- simplicity
- emotional value
- reliability

Key messages include:

"Regala una Vivabox."

"La persona elige la experiencia."

"Nosotros coordinamos todo."

---

# Communication Tone

The tone should feel:

Friendly but elegant.

Vivabox should never sound overly formal.

Avoid bureaucratic language.

The tone should feel conversational and accessible.

---

# Words to Avoid

Certain words should be avoided because they can feel artificial or overly luxurious.

Avoid words such as:

- exclusivo
- lujo
- condiciones

These words can make the brand feel distant or intimidating.

Vivabox should remain premium but approachable.

---

# Core Message

The core message of Vivabox can be summarized as:

You give the gift.  
They choose the experience.

This message must remain clear across all communications.

---

# Brand Visual Identity

The visual identity of Vivabox should reflect the brand personality.

The design should feel:

Minimal  
Elegant  
Warm  
Bright  

White space should be used generously.

The design should never feel crowded.

---

# Color Philosophy

The website and brand identity should use a mostly neutral interface with accent colors.

White/cream/natural paper is the dominant background color.

Accent colors are used sparingly to highlight key elements.

Experience categories may use different colors to represent types of activities.

Examples:

Gastronomía (orange)
Bienestar (blue)
Aventura  (red)
Cultura  (purple)
Estancias (green)

These colors should be used carefully to avoid visual overload.

---

# Typography

Vivabox uses a modern sans-serif typography.

The typeface should feel:

Clean  
Elegant  
Readable  

Typography must remain highly legible on mobile devices.

---

# Photography Style

Images are essential to communicate the experience behind the gift.

Photography should focus on:

- real moments
- authentic emotions
- people enjoying experiences

The visual style should feel natural and inspiring.

The goal is to help visitors imagine the experiences.

---

# Role of the Box

The physical Vivabox is a central element of the brand.

It represents the moment of gifting.

The box should appear frequently in visuals.

It reinforces the emotional impact of the gift.

---

# Brand Inspirations

The brand draws inspiration from several well-known companies.

Airbnb  
Inspiration for experience storytelling.

Apple  
Clarity and simplicity of design.

Smartbox  
Product structure and gift concept.

These inspirations should guide the overall direction without copying them directly.

---

# Brand Experience

The Vivabox brand experience should feel consistent across all touchpoints.

From the website to the physical box to the experience itself, the brand should communicate:

Simplicity  
Emotion  
Reliability  

Every interaction should reinforce the idea that Vivabox is a thoughtful and successful gift.
