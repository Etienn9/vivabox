# Vivabox — AI Rules

This document defines the rules that any AI assistant must follow when working on the Vivabox project.

These rules are mandatory and override other suggestions.

---

# Language Rules

The Vivabox website is written in Spanish.

The tone should reflect Colombian Spanish.

The language should feel natural and friendly, not overly formal.

Avoid bureaucratic or corporate language.

---

# Primary Audience

The website primarily speaks to the buyer.

The buyer is someone looking for a gift.

Always prioritize the buyer’s perspective.

The recipient experience happens later and is handled through the activation platform.

---

# Core Product Rule

Vivabox is not a marketplace of activities.

Vivabox is a gift box that unlocks experiences.

The correct hierarchy is:

Gift → Box → Experience

Never present Vivabox as a catalogue of activities.

---

# Experience Presentation Rule

Experiences shown on the website are examples only.

They should inspire visitors but not create expectations of specific activities.

Always communicate them as examples.

Example wording:

"Ejemplos de experiencias."

---

# Brand Tone

Vivabox communication should feel:

- elegant
- warm
- simple
- human

Avoid language that feels:

- overly luxurious
- bureaucratic
- technical

---

# Words to Avoid

Avoid the following words:

exclusivo  
lujo  
condiciones

These words do not match the brand positioning.

Vivabox should feel premium but accessible.

---

# Core Messages

The following messages represent the essence of Vivabox.

"Regala una Vivabox."

"La persona elige la experiencia."

"Nosotros coordinamos todo."

---

# Website Philosophy

The website should remain simple and clear.

Visitors must understand the concept in seconds.

The site should focus on:

- the gift idea
- the simplicity of the process
- the emotional value

Avoid creating a complex catalogue-like experience.

---

# Design Philosophy

The website design should feel:

minimal  
elegant  
warm  
bright  

White space should be used generously.

Avoid cluttered interfaces.

---

# Visual Hierarchy

The correct visual hierarchy is:

Gift  
Box  
Experience

The physical box should appear frequently in visuals.

It reinforces the emotional moment of gifting.

---

# Decision Priority

When multiple design or UX choices are possible, prioritize:

1 Simplicity  
2 Emotional clarity  
3 Trust and reliability

Never prioritize complexity.
