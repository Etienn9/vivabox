# Vivabox — Brand Foundation

## Core Positioning
Vivabox is the safest, simplest way to offer an experience without getting it wrong.

## Core Value Proposition
- The receiver chooses
- Wide selection of experiences
- Simple process
- Reliable partners

## Emotional Drivers
Primary:
- Trust
- Curiosity

Secondary:
- Pleasure
- Relief

## What We Are NOT
- Not luxury intimidating
- Not cheap mass-market
- Not cold corporate

## Brand Personality
Vivabox is like:
A reliable, elegant, warm, organized woman who makes everything flow effortlessly.

## Design Priorities
1. Clarity
2. Trust
3. Simplicity
4. Warmth
5. Aesthetic (last, not first)

## UX Priority Order
1. Perfect understanding
2. Projection / desire
3. Conversion

## Core Message to Always Reinforce
“I will not get it wrong.”
