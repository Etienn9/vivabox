# Vivabox — Imagery Guidelines

## Philosophy
Images are proof, not decoration.

---

## What to Show

- Real people
- Real experiences
- Natural emotions

---

## What to Avoid

- Overly staged images
- Generic stock photos
- Artificial smiles

---

## Style

- Warm light
- Colorful
- Natural environments
- Slight imperfection = good

---

## Composition

- Human-centered
- Action-oriented
- Context visible

---

## Goal

User must think:
“I can see myself there”
