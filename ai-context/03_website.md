# Vivabox — MVP Website Model

## Purpose of the Website

The Vivabox website is designed primarily for the **buyer**.

Its main objective is to answer one simple question:

> **"What gift should I give?"**

The website must communicate, within a few seconds:

- what Vivabox is
- why it is an excellent gift
- how simple it is to use
- why the recipient will love it

The website has one primary goal:

**Convert visitors into Vivabox buyers.**

---

# Primary User

The primary user is the **buyer**.

Someone looking for a gift for:

- birthdays
- anniversaries
- Mother's Day
- Father's Day
- Christmas
- Valentine's Day
- thank-you gifts
- celebrations
- surprises

Every page should speak directly to this person.

The buyer is the customer.

The recipient is the beneficiary.

---

# Secondary Users

Although the buyer is the main audience, the website also serves two additional audiences.

## Recipients

Recipients may visit the website after receiving a Vivabox.

Their primary objective is to activate their gift.

A prominent call-to-action should always be visible:

> **"Activar mi Vivabox"**

This button redirects to the beneficiary web application.

---

## Providers

Experience providers may visit the website to understand the concept and apply to join the Vivabox network.

A discreet footer link is sufficient:

> **"Ofrecer una experiencia"**

The provider journey is secondary during the MVP.

---

# Website Navigation

Navigation should remain extremely simple.

The objective is to minimize decision fatigue and maximize understanding.

Suggested navigation:

- Inicio
- ¿Cómo funciona?
- Regalar Vivabox
- Empresas
- FAQ
- Activar mi Vivabox

---

# Main Pages

## Homepage

The homepage is the most important page of the MVP.

Its mission is to explain the concept in less than 10 seconds and build enough confidence for visitors to purchase.

Suggested structure:

1. Hero
2. How Vivabox works
3. The physical box
4. Experience inspiration
5. Why Vivabox
6. Gift occasions
7. Testimonials
8. FAQ
9. Final call-to-action

Everything should reinforce one message:

> **You give the gift. They choose the experience.**

---

## Product Page

The MVP launches with **one single product**.

Instead of multiple box pages, there is one dedicated product page.

Example URL:

`/regalar`

This page should answer:

- What is included?
- How does it work?
- What kinds of experiences can be chosen?
- Why is it a great gift?
- How much does it cost?

Suggested structure:

- Hero
- What's inside the box
- How it works
- Experience examples
- Gift occasions
- Customer reassurance
- FAQ
- Purchase section

This page is the primary conversion page of the website.

---

# Experience Inspiration

The website should showcase representative experiences to help buyers imagine the possibilities.

Examples:

- Gastronomy
- Wellness
- Adventure
- Culture
- Short stays

Experiences should always be presented as inspiration.

Never as guaranteed offers.

Preferred wording:

> **"Ejemplos de experiencias."**

The complete catalog is only visible after activation.

---

# Gift Occasions

The website should help buyers visualize when Vivabox is the right gift.

Examples include:

- Birthdays
- Anniversaries
- Mother's Day
- Father's Day
- Christmas
- Congratulations
- Thank-you gifts

Dedicated SEO landing pages can be added after the MVP.

---

# Checkout

The checkout should be as short as possible.

Recommended flow:

1. Purchase Vivabox
2. Add an optional personal message
3. Select delivery method
4. Enter buyer information
5. Complete payment

Every unnecessary field should be eliminated.

---

# Gift Personalization

Buyers can include a personal message.

The message is displayed to the recipient.

Personalization should remain optional and lightweight.

---

# Delivery

The MVP focuses on the **physical Vivabox**.

The buyer can choose the delivery address during checkout.

A digital version may be introduced later but is not part of the initial launch.

---

# Activation

Recipients activate their Vivabox using the unique code included inside the box.

Activation takes place in the dedicated beneficiary web application.

The website should always provide a clear access point:

> **"Activar mi Vivabox"**

---

# Companies

A simple page explains how companies can use Vivabox for:

- employee recognition
- client gifts
- incentives
- corporate celebrations

During the MVP, this page serves primarily as a credibility signal.

---

# Provider Page

A dedicated page explains:

- how partnerships work
- the benefits of joining Vivabox
- how providers can apply

This page is informative rather than conversion-focused during the MVP.

---

# Support Pages

The website should include:

- FAQ
- Contact
- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions

These pages reinforce trust and answer common questions.

---

# Website Philosophy

## Simplicity

Visitors should understand Vivabox within seconds.

The core message is always:

> **You give the box. They choose the experience.**

---

## Inspiration

Beautiful photography and carefully selected examples should help visitors imagine the gift.

The website inspires.

It does not overwhelm.

---

## Trust

Every page should reduce uncertainty.

Trust is built through:

- clear explanations
- authentic photography
- carefully curated partners
- transparent policies
- customer testimonials
- premium presentation

---

# MVP Principle

The MVP deliberately avoids complexity.

There is:

- one product
- one clear promise
- one purchase journey
- one activation flow

Every design and UX decision should reinforce this objective:

**Make buying the perfect gift feel effortless.**
