# Vivabox — MVP Product Model

## Overview

Vivabox is a gift product that allows people to offer experiences without choosing the exact activity in advance.

The buyer purchases a Vivabox.
The recipient activates the box and chooses the experience they prefer.

The core value of the product is **the flexibility of choice combined with the emotional impact of giving a physical gift**.

Vivabox is not an activity marketplace.

Vivabox is a **gift box that unlocks a curated selection of experiences**.

For the MVP, Vivabox launches with **one single product**, making the concept instantly understandable and operationally simple.

---

# MVP Product

The MVP consists of **one physical Vivabox** priced at approximately **COP $200,000**.

Rather than choosing between different price ranges, buyers simply decide whether Vivabox is the right gift.

Inside the box, the recipient will find:

- a unique activation code
- a short explanation of the concept
- a premium presentation
- access to a curated selection of experiences

The objective of the MVP is to validate the customer experience before expanding the product line.

---

# Experience Selection

After activating their Vivabox, the recipient gains access to a curated selection of experiences available for that product (on a separated webapp, different from the website).

The selection includes experiences across different interests, such as:

- Gastronomy
- Wellness
- Adventure
- Culture
- Short stays

The recipient chooses the experience that best matches their preferences.

Vivabox then coordinates the reservation with the selected provider.

---

# Important Rule: Experiences Are Examples

The website must never present experiences as guaranteed items.

Instead, experiences should always be shown as **examples of what may be available**.

Preferred wording:

**"Ejemplos de experiencias."**

The actual experiences available depend on the current partner network and are displayed only after the recipient activates their Vivabox.

This approach keeps expectations realistic while allowing the experience catalog to evolve over time.

---

# Experience Categories

Experiences are organized into broad categories to inspire recipients.

Current categories include:

- Gastronomía
- Bienestar
- Aventura
- Cultura
- Estancias

These categories help users explore the catalog but are not separate products.

---

# Gift Format

## Physical Box

The MVP is launched exclusively as a **physical gift box**.

The box contains:

- an activation code
- simple instructions
- a premium unboxing experience

The physical product reinforces the emotional moment of giving.

---

# Personalization

Buyers can add a personal message to accompany the gift.

Personalization remains intentionally simple.

The focus is on making gifting easy rather than offering endless customization options.

---

# Product Lifecycle

The Vivabox experience follows a simple journey:

1. The buyer purchases a Vivabox.
2. The buyer gives the box to the recipient.
3. The recipient activates the code.
4. The recipient discovers the available experiences.
5. The recipient selects one experience.
6. Vivabox coordinates the reservation.
7. The recipient enjoys the experience.

Every step should feel simple, reassuring, and effortless.

---

# Validity

Each Vivabox is valid for a limited period.

The expected validity for the MVP is:

**6 months from the date of purchase.**

The validity period provides recipients with enough flexibility while encouraging them to enjoy their gift.

---

# Flexibility

Recipients should always feel free to choose the experience that suits them best.

If an experience is temporarily unavailable, Vivabox offers alternative options within the same product.

The objective is simple:

**The gift should always succeed.**

---

# Product Communication Principles

## Speak to the Buyer

The buyer is the decision-maker.

Every product page should answer questions such as:

- Is this a meaningful gift?
- Will the recipient enjoy it?
- Is it easy to give?
- Can I trust the process?

---

## Emphasize the Gift

Vivabox is first and foremost a gift.

The emotional moment of giving the box always comes before the experience itself.

The experience is the continuation of the gift, not the product being sold.

---

## Avoid Marketplace Thinking

Vivabox should never feel like an activity booking platform.

The website should inspire buyers with representative experiences, without overwhelming them with hundreds of options.

The experiences support the gift.
They do not define it.

---

# Product Hierarchy

The product hierarchy is always:

**Gift → Box → Experience**

This principle is fundamental to the Vivabox brand.

Customers are not buying an activity.

They are buying the confidence of giving a gift that the recipient will genuinely enjoy.

---

# Future Evolution

The MVP intentionally focuses on a single product to maximize clarity and operational excellence.

As Vivabox grows, additional price tiers and product ranges may be introduced while preserving the same core promise:

**One gift. One choice. One unforgettable experience.**
