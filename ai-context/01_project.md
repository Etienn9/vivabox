# Vivabox — Project Overview

## What Vivabox Is

Vivabox is a gift box that allows people to offer experiences.

Instead of choosing a specific activity, the buyer gives a Vivabox.
The person who receives it later chooses the experience they prefer.

Vivabox transforms the difficulty of choosing a gift into a simple and reliable solution.

For the MVP, Vivabox launches with a single gift box priced at approximately **COP $200,000**. The recipient can choose from a curated selection of experiences included in that box.

Main promise:

**"El regalo que siempre acierta."**

---

# The Problem Vivabox Solves

Choosing a gift is often difficult.

People want to give something:

- original
- meaningful
- memorable

But they often do not know:

- what the person wants
- what activity they would enjoy
- when they would be available

As a result, many gifts fail or feel impersonal.

Vivabox solves this problem by allowing the recipient to choose the experience themselves.

The buyer gives the idea.
The recipient chooses the moment.

---

# The Core Concept

Vivabox is not an activity marketplace.

Vivabox is a **gift that turns into an experience**.

The experience is chosen later by the person who receives the box.

Sequence:

Gift → Surprise → Experience

The gift moment and the experience moment are separated.

This makes the gift both emotional and flexible.

---

# The Three Actors

Vivabox involves three different actors.

These actors must always be clearly distinguished.

## Buyer

The buyer is the person who purchases the Vivabox.

They want to give a gift that:

- feels special
- is easy to offer
- will be appreciated

The website primarily speaks to the buyer.

The buyer must feel confident that the gift will succeed.

---

## Recipient

The recipient is the person who receives the Vivabox.

They activate the code and choose the experience they prefer.

The recipient never sees the price of the box.

Their experience must feel:

- free
- personal
- exciting

The recipient becomes the emotional ambassador of the brand.

---

## Provider

The provider is the partner business that delivers the experience.

Examples:

- restaurants
- spas
- adventure companies
- hotels
- cultural experiences

Providers are partners, not just suppliers.

They must feel respected, supported, and fairly compensated.

---

# MVP Product

The initial launch focuses on **one single Vivabox**.

This allows the team to validate the concept, refine operations, and deliver an exceptional customer experience before expanding the product range.

Approximate retail price:

**COP $200,000**

The box includes a curated selection of experiences across multiple categories, allowing the recipient to choose the one they prefer.

Example categories:

- Gastronomy
- Wellness
- Adventure
- Culture
- Short stays

Future versions of Vivabox may introduce additional price tiers, but the MVP intentionally focuses on one product.

---

# Emotional Promise

Vivabox must always communicate the following ideas:

1. This is a great gift idea.
2. The recipient will love it.
3. It is simple to use.
4. The experience will be memorable.

The buyer should feel relief and confidence.

---

# Key Principles

Vivabox is built on three principles.

## Simplicity

The concept must be understood in seconds.

A visitor should immediately understand:

> "You give the box. They choose the experience."

---

## Reliability

The buyer must feel confident that the gift will succeed.

This means:

- curated experiences
- trusted partners
- clear activation process
- responsive customer support

---

## Warmth

Vivabox should feel human and emotional.

Not corporate.

Not technical.

The brand should feel:

- elegant
- friendly
- generous

---

# The Gift Philosophy

Vivabox is fundamentally about gifting.

It is not about booking activities.

The product hierarchy is always:

Gift → Box → Experience

Never the opposite.

The experience is the outcome of the gift.

---

# Geographic Focus

The MVP launches in:

- Bogotá
- Cundinamarca

Future expansion may include other regions of Colombia.

---

Vivabox targets:

- middle and upper-middle class consumers
- urban professionals
- people looking for thoughtful gifts
- companies looking for meaningful corporate gifts

Typical occasions include:

- birthdays
- anniversaries
- Valentine's Day
- Mother's Day
- Father's Day
- Christmas
- thank-you gifts
- celebrations
- surprises

---

# Website Purpose

The website is primarily designed for the buyer.

Its objective is to answer one question:

> **"What gift should I give?"**

The website must quickly communicate:

- the concept
- the simplicity
- the emotional value
- the reliability

The site should guide visitors toward purchasing a Vivabox.

---

# Brand Personality

Vivabox should feel:

- Elegant
- Warm
- Modern
- Accessible

The brand should never feel:

- bureaucratic
- complicated
- impersonal

---

# Important Rule

Vivabox must never present itself as a catalogue of activities.

The product is the gift.

The experience is what the recipient chooses afterward.

This distinction is essential for the brand.

---

# Future Vision

The MVP intentionally launches with a single product to maximize clarity and operational excellence.

Once product-market fit is validated, Vivabox will progressively expand into multiple price tiers and a broader portfolio of curated experiences while maintaining the same promise:

**The easiest way to give an unforgettable experience.**
