# Vivabox — Website UX

> Source of truth for: site structure, pages, navigation, checkout flow.
> Audience roles → see `00_foundation.md`. Tone/copy → see `02_brand-voice.md`.

## Purpose

Answer, within seconds: **"What gift should I give?"** Convert visitors into buyers.

## Audience on the Site

- **Buyer** (primary) — every page speaks to them.
- **Recipient** (secondary) — needs a persistent CTA: **"Activar mi Vivabox"** → redirects to the beneficiary web app.
- **Provider** (secondary) — one discreet footer link: **"Ofrecer una experiencia."**

## Navigation

Inicio · ¿Cómo funciona? · Regalar Vivabox · Empresas · FAQ · Activar mi Vivabox

Keep it minimal — minimize decision fatigue.

## Homepage

Most important page. Explain the concept in under 10 seconds.

1. Hero
2. How Vivabox works
3. The physical box
4. Experience inspiration
5. Why Vivabox
6. Gift occasions
7. Testimonials
8. FAQ
9. Final CTA

## Product Page (`/regalar`)

Single dedicated page (MVP has one product — no multi-box catalog). Must answer: what's included, how it works, what kinds of experiences, why it's a great gift, cost.

Structure: Hero → What's inside the box → How it works → Experience examples → Gift occasions → Customer reassurance → FAQ → Purchase section.

This is the primary conversion page.

## Experience Inspiration & Gift Occasions

Show representative experiences and occasions (birthdays, anniversaries, Mother's/Father's Day, Christmas, congratulations, thank-you gifts) to help buyers visualize use cases. Always framed as inspiration, per the "Ejemplos de experiencias" rule in `02_brand-voice.md`. SEO landing pages per occasion can come post-MVP.

## Checkout (minimize fields)

1. Purchase Vivabox
2. Optional personal message
3. Select delivery method
4. Buyer information
5. Payment

## Delivery & Activation

- MVP ships the **physical box only**; buyer chooses delivery address at checkout.
- Recipient activates via the code inside the box, in the beneficiary web app.
- Website always surfaces the "Activar mi Vivabox" entry point.

## Companies Page

Explains employee recognition, client gifts, incentives, corporate celebrations. At MVP, functions mainly as a credibility signal, not a conversion page.

## Provider Page

Informative, not conversion-focused at MVP: how partnerships work, benefits, how to apply.

## Support Pages

FAQ · Contact · Privacy Policy · Terms & Conditions.

## MVP Scope Guardrail

One product · one clear promise · one purchase journey · one activation flow. Resist adding complexity.
