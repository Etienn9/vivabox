# Product Philosophy

Vivabox is not an experience marketplace.

Vivabox is a **gift product** designed to make offering an experience simple, memorable and reliable.

The product is intentionally built around one principle:

> **A great experience deserves a real gift.**

People don't remember receiving a QR code or an email.
They remember someone handing them a gift.

The physical box is therefore **part of the product**, not its packaging.

It creates the emotional moment of giving before the experience even begins.

Everything else exists to make sure the gift succeeds.

---

# MVP Product

Launch product:

- One physical Vivabox
- Approx. COP $200,000
- One clear buying decision:
  **"Do I want to give a Vivabox?"**
  — not
  **"Which Vivabox should I choose?"**

The MVP intentionally avoids multiple products, tiers or unnecessary complexity.

---

# What's Inside

Each Vivabox contains:

- Premium physical presentation
- Personal message (optional)
- Short explanation of the concept
- Unique activation code

The recipient then accesses a separate activation webapp where they:

- discover available experiences;
- choose their favourite;
- request preferred dates;
- let Vivabox coordinate the reservation.

The website sells the gift.

The activation platform manages the experience.

These are two different products for two different users.

---

# Why a Physical Box?

Removing the box would reduce logistics costs.

It would also fundamentally change the product.

Without the box, Vivabox becomes another digital voucher.

The physical box:

- creates the gifting ritual;
- increases perceived value;
- makes the surprise tangible;
- differentiates Vivabox from traditional gift cards;
- reinforces the hierarchy:

> Gift → Box → Experience

The experience may happen weeks later.

The gift happens today.

# Product Lifecycle

1. Buyer purchases a Vivabox.
2. Buyer gives the box.
3. Recipient opens the gift.
4. Recipient activates the code.
5. Recipient discovers available experiences.
6. Recipient chooses one experience.
7. Vivabox coordinates the booking.
8. Recipient enjoys the experience.

The emotional sequence always remains:

**Gift → Surprise → Experience**

---

# Flexibility Rule

Experiences may evolve over time.

The promise never changes.

If one experience becomes unavailable, Vivabox proposes suitable alternatives.

The objective is never to preserve a catalogue.

The objective is to preserve the success of the gift.

---

# Personalization

The buyer may include a short personal message.

Its purpose is not customization.

Its purpose is to reinforce the emotional moment when the box is opened.

---

# Validity

6 months from date of purchase.

---

# Future Evolution

Future versions may introduce:

- additional price tiers;
- digital-only products;
- new product ranges.

Those evolutions must never compromise the original promise:

**One gift. One choice. One unforgettable experience.**
