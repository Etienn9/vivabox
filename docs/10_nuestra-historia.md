# Purpose

This page is **not** an "About Us" page.

It is a **trust-building page**.

Its role is to humanize Vivabox by explaining why the company exists, how it was built and what commitments guide every decision.

It should increase confidence without becoming corporate.

---

# Position inside the website

Homepage

↓

Product Page

↓

Checkout

↓

Nuestra historia

The page is accessible from the navigation and footer.

It is **not** part of the purchase funnel.

It supports trust before or after purchase.

---

# The Question this Page Answers

The Homepage answers:

> Why Vivabox?

The Product Page answers:

> Why buy this Vivabox?

The Checkout answers:

> How do I buy it?

This page answers:

> **Why can I trust the people behind Vivabox?**

---

# Primary Objective

Humanize the brand.

Not by talking about the founders.

But by explaining:

- why Vivabox exists;
- what problem it solves;
- how it is built;
- what principles guide every decision.

The founders are part of the story.

They are **not** the story.

---

# Brand Principles

This page must feel:

✓ Warm

✓ Honest

✓ Humble

✓ Human

✓ Calm

It must never feel:

✗ Corporate

✗ Self-congratulatory

✗ Startup pitch

✗ Biography

✗ LinkedIn

# Important Principle

The page is about Vivabox.

Not about two French founders.

The reader should finish the page thinking:

> "These people genuinely care about creating a better way to give gifts."

Not:

> "These are two French entrepreneurs."
