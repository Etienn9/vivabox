# Vivabox — Foundation

> Source of truth for: positioning, value proposition, brand personality, core principles, target audience.
> Other files reference this one instead of repeating it.

## What Vivabox Is

A gift box that unlocks a curated selection of experiences. The buyer gives the box; the recipient later chooses the experience.

**Core hierarchy (never reverse this):**

```
Gift → Box → Experience
```

Vivabox is **not** an activity marketplace. The experience is the outcome of the gift, not the product being sold.

## Brand Promise

**"El regalo que siempre acierta."**

Core message to reinforce everywhere: **"I will not get it wrong."**
Public-facing version: **"You give the box. They choose the experience."**

## Positioning

The safest, simplest way to offer an experience without getting it wrong.

**Premium but accessible** — not luxury/intimidating, not cheap/mass-market, not cold/corporate.

## Value Proposition

- The receiver chooses
- Wide selection of experiences
- Simple process
- Reliable partners

## Brand Personality

Vivabox is like: *a reliable, elegant, warm, organized woman who makes everything flow effortlessly.*

| Is | Is never |
|---|---|
| Elegant | Corporate / bureaucratic |
| Warm | Technical / complicated |
| Modern | Cold |
| Human / accessible | Intimidating |

## Emotional Drivers

- **Primary:** Trust, Curiosity
- **Secondary:** Pleasure, Relief

Emotional sequence: **Gift → Surprise → Experience**
When someone discovers Vivabox: *"Qué buena idea de regalo."*
When someone receives it: Sorpresa, Emoción, Curiosidad.

## Design & Decision Priorities

1. Clarity / Simplicity
2. Trust / Emotional clarity
3. Reliability
4. Warmth
5. Aesthetic (last, not first)

**UX priority order:** Perfect understanding → Projection/desire → Conversion

## The Three Actors

| Actor | Role | Needs to feel |
|---|---|---|
| **Buyer** | Purchases the box; primary audience of the website | Confident the gift will succeed |
| **Recipient** | Activates the code, chooses the experience (never sees the price) | Free, personal, excited |
| **Provider** | Partner business delivering the experience | Respected, supported, fairly compensated |

## Target Audience

Middle and upper-middle class consumers, urban professionals, couples, friends/family, companies (corporate gifting). Occasions: birthdays, anniversaries, Valentine's Day, Mother's/Father's Day, Christmas, thank-you gifts, celebrations, surprises.

**Geographic focus (MVP):** Bogotá, Cundinamarca. Future: other regions of Colombia.

## Brand Inspirations

- **Airbnb** — experience storytelling
- **Apple** — clarity and simplicity of design
- **Smartbox** — product structure and gift concept

Guide direction; do not copy directly.
