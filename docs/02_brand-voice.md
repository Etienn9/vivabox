# Vivabox — Brand Voice

> Source of truth for: tone, language rules, copywriting, key messages.
> Brand personality/positioning → see `00_foundation.md`.

## Language

- Website written in **Spanish**, Colombian tone.
- Natural and friendly, never bureaucratic or corporate.
- Short sentences preferred.

## Tone

Friendly but elegant. Conversational and accessible — never overly formal.

## Words to Avoid

`exclusivo` · `lujo` · `condiciones`

These make the brand feel distant or intimidating. Vivabox is premium but approachable.

## Core Messages (use verbatim where relevant)

- "Regala una Vivabox."
- "La persona elige la experiencia."
- "Nosotros coordinamos todo."

## Experience Presentation Rule

Experiences shown on the website are **examples only**, never guaranteed items. This keeps expectations realistic while the catalog evolves with the partner network.

Required wording: **"Ejemplos de experiencias."**

Full catalog is visible only after activation (separate webapp).

## Copywriting Priorities

Always speak to the **buyer** (see Three Actors in `00_foundation.md`). Every line should answer, directly or indirectly:

- Is this a meaningful gift?
- Will the recipient enjoy it?
- Is it easy to give?
- Can I trust the process?

Priority order: simplicity → emotional value → reliability.
